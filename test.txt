Model Risk Management – Key Process Flow (Summary)
Definition:

Model: Input (expert judgment, qualitative, quantitative) → Process → Output (quantitative, qualitative)

A Deterministic Quantitative Method (DQM) that has no uncertainty is NOT a Model.

Model includes: model risk + assumptions + uncertainty.

DQM needs governance but has no uncertainty.

QM Classification:

QM (Quantitative Method) → Identification → Classification → Onboarding → GMIS

Classification outcomes: Model, DQM, or NMND (Neither Model Nor DQM).

Lifecycle for a Model
If classified as a Model:

Tiering → assign a risk tier (1–5) based on Materiality, Complexity, Reliance.

Development → design, build, test.

Testing → development tests and implementation testing.

Validation → independent review by 2LOD (MRM) → outputs: MRIs & IMLs.

Approval → by Model Sponsor (1LOD) and Model Risk Steward (2LOD) → outcome: Approved / Conditional / Rejected / Approved with Exception.

Go-Live → model is deployed and used in production.

Special Considerations for AI-based QMs (Classification)
When classifying an AI-based Quantitative Method, consider:

Uncertainty from AI output combined with human judgment – who makes the final decision?

Ground truth in subjective issues – is there a clear correct answer?

Reliance – how much do business decisions rely on the AI output?

Proximity to business decisions – e.g., AI → summary → analyst → committee → decision (how close is it to the final decision?).

AI models are expected to be reviewed by both MRL and MRS.

Inventory & Onboarding
QMIO = Quantitative Method Identification and Onboarding tool.

GMIS = Group Model Inventory System.

After classification, a record is created in GMIS.

Timeline: Onboarding/identification process should be completed within 90 days.

Model Statuses in GMIS:

Planning (only for models)

Delivery (development started)

In-use (approved and deployed)

Decommissioned

Single-use models: must be decommissioned within 30 working days after use.

Annual Inventory Confirmation
1LOD (GB/GI): Reviews inventory completeness and accuracy, reports to governance forum.

2LOD (MRM): Performs independent review & challenge on accuracy/completeness, using automated tests and sampling.

Key Roles
QMIO Requestor / Approver – 1LOD roles for identification/onboarding.

Model Owner – accountable for model lifecycle, inventory, action plans, PMAs.

Model Developer – builds model, creates monitoring plan.

Model Sponsor – 1LOD approval for use.

MRL (Model Risk Lead) – 2LOD, manages validation schedule, reviews tiers and outcomes.

Model Validator – 2LOD, performs independent validation and issue management.

MRS (Model Risk Steward) – 2LOD, final approval for model use, overrides, escalations.

Post-Model Adjustments (PMA) & Monitoring
PMA: manual adjustment to model output due to known limitation; must be documented and independently reviewed.

Monitoring: Model Developer defines monitoring plan with quantitative tests and thresholds; Model Owner executes it; results uploaded to GMIS; Validator reviews breaches.

Persistent breach may trigger MRI or accelerate Revalidation.

Revalidation & Decommissioning
Revalidation: periodic (usually annual) independent review to confirm model remains fit for purpose; less detailed than initial validation.

Decommissioning: Model Owner requests, Model Sponsor approves, status set to Decommissioned in GMIS. For single-use, within 30 days.

Policy Non-Compliance
Non-compliance may be approval-related (need Approval with Exception) or material/exceptional (need Dispensation via Group GMS system).

Model Owner classifies, informs stakeholders, and follows remediation plan.
