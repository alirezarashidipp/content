Model Risk Lifecycle

1LOD owns & runs · 2LOD (MRM) reviews & challenges
1
Identify & classify Register the QM in QMIO → Model, DQM or NMND. Unsure? Tool questions; still not determined → MRL / MRS decide. NMND → end (document)
2
Onboard & inventory Record in GMIS and keep it accurate and up to date. DQM → govern & monitor per DQM policy
3
Tier Assign Tier 1–5 from materiality, complexity and reliance.
4
Develop & document Build, test and document the model; set the monitoring plan.
5
Validate Independent 2LOD validation; MRIs / IMLs raised and remediated.
6
Approve Model Sponsor + MRS approve use — possibly with conditions.
7
Monitor Track performance; persistent breaches are escalated.
8
Revalidate Periodic independent review ↺ repeats until retirement.
9
Decommission Retire the model and update GMIS (Single Use: 30 working days).
END OF LIFECYCLE
Full detail: Group Model Risk Procedure v5.2, §§1–13
